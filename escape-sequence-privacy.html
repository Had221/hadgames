Privacy Policy — Escape Sequence

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers Escape Sequence, a puzzle game by Had Games, and the data it handles.

What we collect

We don't collect anything ourselves. Escape Sequence has no accounts and no sign-in. Your level, lives, hints and purchases are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also stored in your own iCloud account so you can restore it on a new device or after reinstalling. We can't access that copy, and we have no servers of our own. The game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, including its advertising identifier, IP address and general usage data. The game shows Apple's tracking prompt before any of this happens:

  • If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • If you decline, ads are not personalized and no cross-app identifier is used.

The game works the same either way. Google describes how it handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the UK and the European Economic Area, Google's consent form appears before any ads are requested. You can change your answer at any time from inside the game.

Purchases

Purchases (unlimited lives, removing ads and hints) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Escape Sequence is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
