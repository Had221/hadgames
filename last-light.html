Last Light

An idle game for iPhone and iPad

You are a self-replicating probe swarm sent to a dead solar system. Harvest matter, build more probes and consume the system, then launch a seed probe to the next star with a better blueprint. Nobody remembers who launched you, or why.

How it plays

  • Instead of filling the screen up, you take a solar system apart. The more you consume, the less of it is left.
  • Story fragments turn up as you play, and they change how you see what's on screen.
  • Everything is drawn in code: orbits, dust and light, with no sprites or imported art.

Launching and the Atlas

  • Prestige is launching. You send a seed probe onward with an improved blueprint, and choose which star it goes to.
  • Automation is the swarm becoming independent, and the story builds on that.
  • The Atlas records every system you consume, and there are records to chase with strategies you might not otherwise try.

Offline earnings, ads and your save

  • The swarm earns for up to twelve hours while you're away.
  • Rewarded ads are optional, and a one-time purchase removes the banner permanently.
  • Progress backs up to your own iCloud, with no account to create.
