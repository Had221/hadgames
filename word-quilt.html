Word Quilt: Stitch & Spell

// a cozy word puzzle — for iPhone and iPad

Swipe letters on a wheel to fill a crossword. Every puzzle you solve stitches another patch onto a hand-drawn scene, until you've quilted a postcard from somewhere worth visiting — and then you set off for the next one.

A puzzle that builds something

  • Thirty destinations, each one a scene that assembles patch by patch as you play. The puzzles aren't decorated with a picture — they draw it.
  • Over a thousand levels, generated deterministically from curated word lists, so the journey is the same one for everybody and every board is solvable.
  • Every scene is drawn in code. Not one photograph — each place is built from shapes, which is why the patches can stitch into it.

Cozy, and it means it

  • No timers and no lives. Nothing expires, nothing locks you out, nothing asks you to come back in four hours.
  • Extra words count for something. The ones you find that aren't in the crossword still earn their keep.
  • Play it one puzzle at a time or lose an evening to it. It's the same game either way.

Yours, wherever you play

  • Progress follows your Apple ID to iCloud, with no account and no sign-up.
  • Optional ads and a small shop — nothing is locked behind either.
