Privacy Policy — Pocket Terrarium

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers Pocket Terrarium, an idle game by Had Games, and the data it handles.

What we collect

Pocket Terrarium has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your terrariums, critters and Bloom are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also stored in your own iCloud account so you can restore it on a new device. We can't access that copy.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob: you choose to watch one in exchange for a boost. There are no banners or interstitials. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect information from your device, including its advertising identifier, IP address and general usage data.

Pocket Terrarium requests non-personalized ads only. It does not use your advertising identifier to build a profile of you across apps, so the game never shows Apple's tracking prompt. Google describes how it handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the UK and the European Economic Area, Google's consent form appears before any ads are requested. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is used: how many people play, which screens they open, how far into the collection they get, and their country, which is worked out from the IP address. We do not store IP addresses ourselves. The data is tied to an identifier created for this app only. It is not your name, email address, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and we do not combine it with anything that identifies you.

We only look at totals, such as how many players reached their fourth terrarium.

Analytics stays off until you answer Google's consent form, and stays off if you decline or if the form can't be shown. Apple's tracking prompt only covers advertising identifiers, so analytics asks for consent separately. Google describes how it handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The game has one optional purchase. It is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. No third-party purchase or receipt service is involved, nothing about your purchase is sent to us, and we never see your payment details. The game keeps a single note in your save that you own the upgrade.

Children

Pocket Terrarium is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
