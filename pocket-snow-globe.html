Pocket Snow Globe

// a cozy idle game — for iPhone and iPad

Stir up a flurry, watch it settle, and slowly fill a little glass world with the things that belong in it. Then move on to the next one. There are eighteen.

Eighteen little worlds

  • Real places and imagined ones. Paris, London, New York, Tokyo, Rome, Cairo, New Orleans, San Francisco, Atlanta, Birmingham — and then Atlantis, Avalon, Mount Olympus, El Dorado.
  • Every scene is drawn in code. Not one photograph, not one imported image — every tower, awning and drift is a shape placed by hand, which is why the snow settles on all of it properly.
  • Each globe has its own four pieces to find, its own sky, and its own stand — brass, walnut, silver, frosted glass.

A quiet loop

  • Stir the globe for snowflakes, or just leave it. Pieces you place keep making snowflakes while the app is closed.
  • Advance when a globe is full, and keep a permanent bonus for the rest of the winter.
  • Begin a New Winter when it slows down: bank everything as holiday spirit and spend it in the Workshop on upgrades that carry into every winter after.

Kind about it

  • No banners, no interrupting ads. The only ads are optional rewarded ones you have to go and tap. Nothing is locked behind them.
  • One purchase, if you want it — a permanent doubling. That's the whole shop.
  • No timers, no energy, no streak you can break. It is a snow globe. It waits.
  • Your globes follow you. Progress backs up to your own iCloud with no account and no sign-up, so a new phone picks up where you left off.

Made to be looked at

Take a picture of any globe you've filled and share it — the whole scene, the way you built it.
