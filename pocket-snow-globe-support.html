Support — Pocket Snow Globe

Help and contact

Pocket Snow Globe is an idle game for iPhone and iPad by Had Games. If your question isn't answered here, email us.

Common questions

Does the game keep earning while it's closed?
Yes. Your pieces keep making snowflakes for up to two hours while the app is closed. Each level of the Long Winter upgrade in the Workshop adds two more hours.

What do I keep when I start a New Winter?
Your holiday spirit, your Workshop upgrades and your collection record. Your snowflakes, pieces and globe progress reset.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Yes, through iCloud, with no account to create. Install the game on another device signed in to the same Apple ID and your save comes across. iCloud needs to be turned on for Pocket Snow Globe in Settings.

My progress is gone.
Reinstall the game on a device signed in to your Apple ID and your iCloud save restores automatically. If your device and iCloud have different saves, the game keeps the one with more progress. If your save doesn't come back, email us before starting over.

I bought the Golden Snow Shaker but don't have it.
Go to Menu → Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore in the shop. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so it works on any device signed in to that account. If it still doesn't show up, close the app completely and reopen it, and email us if that doesn't fix it.

The falling snow bothers me.
Turn on Menu → Settings → Reduce motion. The snow and the scene stop moving, and nothing else in the game changes.

Can I make it snow all the time?
Yes. Turn on Menu → Settings → Always snowing and choose light or heavy snow. While it's on, tapping no longer changes the snowfall.

I'm in the UK or the EU and want to change my privacy choices.
Menu → Settings → Privacy options opens Google's consent form again. Your new choice applies right away.

I don't want personalized ads.
Decline Apple's tracking prompt, or turn tracking off later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You'll see the same optional ads, but they won't be personalized.

Something looks wrong or isn't working.
Email us a screenshot, along with your device model and iOS version.
