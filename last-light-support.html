Support — Last Light

Help and contact

Last Light is an idle game for iPhone and iPad by Had Games, about a self-replicating probe swarm consuming solar systems. If your question isn't answered here, email us.

Common questions

Does my swarm work while the app is closed?
Yes. Up to 12 hours banks automatically, and when you come back an optional rewarded ad doubles what was banked.

What do I keep when I launch?
Your Blueprint Data and everything you've bought with it. Your probes, your matter and the system itself are used up.

I bought Remove Ads and need it on a new device.
Purchases restore automatically when the game first opens. If the banner is still there, close the app completely and reopen it once.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Yes, through iCloud, with no account to create. Install Last Light on a new device signed in to the same Apple ID and your swarm comes across. iCloud needs to be turned on for Last Light in Settings.

My progress is gone.
Reinstall the game on a device signed in to your Apple ID and your iCloud backup restores automatically. If it doesn't, email us.
