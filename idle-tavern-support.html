Support — Idle Tavern: Dungeon Delvers

Help and contact

Idle Tavern is a fantasy idle game for iPhone by Had Games, about running a tavern and the dungeon below it. If your question isn't answered here, email us.

Common questions

Does the tavern earn while the app is closed?

Yes. Up to two hours banks automatically at a reduced rate, and a summary shows what you earned when you come back. An optional rewarded ad doubles it.

What do I keep through an Echo Dive?

Your collection, Renown, Gems, Echoes, Echo perks, Ascendant gear and Trophy Shelf. Floors, gold, the basement and the tavern rooms reset, and you get Echoes in return.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, through iCloud, with no account to create. Install Idle Tavern on another device signed in to the same Apple ID and your tavern comes across. iCloud needs to be turned on for Idle Tavern in Settings.

My progress is gone.

Reinstall the game on a device signed in to your Apple ID and the iCloud backup restores automatically. If it doesn't, email us before starting over.

I bought something and it didn't arrive, or I'm on a new device.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Permanent 2× Income re-applies itself. If it still doesn't show, email us with the date of purchase.

Are there forced ads?

No. Every ad in the game is optional: you tap a button to watch one for a reward, and nothing interrupts play.

I'm in the UK or the EU and want to change my ad consent.

Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options to reopen the consent form and change your answer.

I want to see the tutorial again.

Settings → How to play replays the first-run tour at any time.

Contact

Email haddanw@gmail.com with bugs, balance problems or ideas. You can also use Send feedback in the game's settings, which fills in your app version for us. For bugs, include your device model and iOS version.
