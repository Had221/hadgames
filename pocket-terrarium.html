Pocket Terrarium

An idle garden game for iPhone and iPad

Grow twenty terrariums in glass jars, from a mossy glen with a waterfall to a cave full of amethyst. Tap to water, buy plants for each jar, and let them keep growing while the app is closed.

The terrariums

  • Each jar is drawn on its own: a lavender field with a windmill, a lava chamber lit from below, a Japanese stroll garden behind a temple wall.
  • The plants and stones are real ones, such as maple leaves, fly agarics and amethyst.
  • Every jar goes from day to night, and the light changes as it does.

Critters

  • Sixty critters visit the jars they belong to, on their own schedule. Tap one to befriend it, and it stays in your collection.
  • Each critter you befriend makes the whole garden grow a little faster.
  • Some are common and some are rare.

How it plays

  • Your garden keeps growing while you're away, and shows you what it earned when you come back.
  • When you're ready, reseed: trade the current Journey for Bloom and spend it in the Greenhouse on permanent upgrades.
  • The only ads are optional rewarded ones. There are no banners and no interstitials.
  • There is one optional purchase, and nothing in the game requires it.
  • Saves sync through your own iCloud, with no account to make.
