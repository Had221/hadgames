Pocket Terrarium

// cozy idle garden — for iPhone & iPad

Twenty tiny worlds under glass, from a mossy glen with a waterfall to a cave full of amethyst. Tap to water, plant what grows there, and let it keep growing while you're away. No timers to miss, no streak to break, nothing that punishes you for closing the app.

Twenty jars, each its own place

  • Every terrarium is hand-drawn — a lavender field with a windmill, a lava chamber lit from below, a Japanese stroll garden behind a temple wall. None of them are recolours of each other.
  • Plants you actually recognise. Real maple leaves, real fly agarics, real amethyst. The fantasy lives in the light, not in the shapes.
  • Day turns to night in every jar, and the light changes what you're looking at.

Sixty-one critters to meet

  • Creatures visit the jar they belong to, on their own schedule. Tap one to befriend it — that's the whole interaction, and it's permanent.
  • Each one you meet makes the whole garden grow a little faster.
  • Some are common. Some you'll wait for.

Idle, and honest about it

  • Grows while you're away, and tells you plainly what it earned while you were gone.
  • Reseed when you're ready — trade this Journey's garden for Bloom, and spend it in the Greenhouse on things that last forever.
  • Rewarded ads only, always optional. No banners, no interstitials, and no ad you didn't choose to watch.
  • One optional purchase, and nothing is gated behind it.
  • Your garden follows you — saves sync through your own iCloud, with no account to make.
