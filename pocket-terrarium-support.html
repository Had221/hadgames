Support — Pocket Terrarium

Help and contact

Pocket Terrarium is an idle garden game for iPhone and iPad by Had Games. If your question isn't answered here, email us.

Common questions

Does my garden grow while the app is closed?
Yes. Up to 3 hours banks automatically, and the Greenhouse can extend that. Anything past the cap can be topped up with an optional rewarded ad.

What do I keep when I reseed?
Your Bloom and everything you've bought with it in the Greenhouse. The current Journey's terrariums and dew reset.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Yes, through iCloud, with no account to create. Install Pocket Terrarium on another device signed in to the same Apple ID and your garden comes across. iCloud needs to be turned on for Pocket Terrarium in Settings.

My progress is gone.
Reinstall the game on a device signed in to your Apple ID and your iCloud backup restores automatically. If it doesn't, email us before starting over.

I bought the Golden Watering Can and need it on a new device.
Open the menu, tap Shop, and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so it comes back at no charge.

A critter won't appear.
Critters visit on their own schedule, and only in the terrarium they belong to. Leave that jar on screen for a while. Some are rarer than others.

Can I turn off the motion?
Yes. Settings has a Reduce Motion switch that stops the ambient animation, and a Haptics switch.
