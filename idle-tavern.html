Idle Tavern: Dungeon Delvers

// cozy fantasy RPG & tycoon — for iPhone

The Crooked Hearth is a perfectly ordinary tavern, apart from the dungeon under the cellar. Send your delvers down, and watch every coin they win travel the whole way back up to your pocket.

A tavern on top, a machine below

  • Watch the whole pipeline. Chest to bucket to counting bench to your pocket. Nothing is abstract — if the gold moved, you saw it move.
  • Forge every hero's gear in their own floor's rare currency. A fresh delver fells two monsters of five; the day their blade one-shots the whole row is your doing.
  • Clear the bottlenecks. When loot arrives faster than the basement can sort it, the pipeline backs up — and fixing that is the real game.

A cast worth knowing

  • 26 hand-drawn regulars — heroes, staff, and the managers who run floors for you, including a napping orange cat and two very good dogs.
  • 28 rare relics with a Trophy Shelf to fill, and a wandering trader who barters for your duplicates.

Dive, and dive again

  • Prestige with an Echo Dive and surface in one of ten Echo-Verses — alternate taverns where the same faces greet you in that world's colours.
  • Earn while you're away. The Below keeps working when you don't.

Free to play, kind about it: optional rewarded ads and a small shop. No forced videos, no energy timers, no pressure. Pull up a stool.
