Idle Tavern: Dungeon Delvers

A fantasy idle RPG and tycoon game for iPhone

The Crooked Hearth is an ordinary tavern with a dungeon under the cellar. Send your delvers down, and every coin they find is carried back up through the basement to you.

The tavern and the dungeon

  • You can follow every coin: from the chest, to the bucket, to the counting bench, to your pocket.
  • Forge each hero's gear with the rare currency from their own floor. A new delver kills two monsters out of five. With better gear, one swing clears the whole row.
  • When loot comes in faster than the basement can sort it, the line backs up. Clearing those bottlenecks is most of the game.

Characters and relics

  • 26 hand-drawn regulars: heroes, staff, and the managers who run floors for you, including an orange cat and two dogs.
  • 28 rare relics for the Trophy Shelf, and a travelling trader who swaps your duplicates.

Prestige and offline earnings

  • An Echo Dive resets your run and sends you to one of ten Echo-Verses: alternate taverns with the same characters in different colours.
  • The dungeon keeps earning while you're away.

Free to play, with optional rewarded ads and a small shop. There are no forced videos and no energy timers.
